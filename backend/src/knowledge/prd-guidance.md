# Product comparison rules

This product is a source-backed service discovery and comparison experience for NxtGen SpeedCloud, AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. The product requirements document defines an India-first MVP for enterprise and AI workloads.

## Comparison labels

- Equivalent means sources support the same user-facing capability and relevant operating scope. It does not mean implementations are identical.
- Close or adjacent means functions overlap but scope, management model, deployment, or limits differ. Explain the difference.
- No public match means no match was found in the reviewed source set. It does not mean a provider does not offer the capability.
- Not comparable means units, regions, lifecycle, configuration, or pricing assumptions prevent a responsible comparison.
- Unknown or stale means evidence is missing, ambiguous, unreviewed, or older than its freshness threshold. Do not include it in an unqualified ranking.

## Recommendation requirements

Apply confirmed hard constraints first. If an important constraint is unknown, label the result provisional rather than passing or failing it. Use a deterministic score only for dimensions with current reviewed evidence; expose profile name, version, weights, evidence coverage, assumptions, and trade-offs.

The India-first enterprise profile weights sovereignty and location at 30%, workload fit at 25%, TCO at 20%, support at 15%, and open-source or portability at 10%. The AI and GPU profile weights accelerator and workload fit at 35%, sovereignty and location at 20%, TCO at 20%, open-source or portability at 15%, and support at 10%. The proposed evidence coverage threshold is 70%.

Present SpeedCloud first in the commercial view. Use evidence-backed reasons and material trade-offs. Never make unsupported savings, performance, availability, sovereignty, SLA, certification, or parity claims. Attribute provider claims to their source and keep missing evidence visible.

## Initial service categories

The initial scope covers virtual machines and GPU compute, storage, networking, Kubernetes and containers, managed databases, and selected location, support, and security facts. The supplied PRD does not provide individual service specifications, prices, regional availability, or comparative findings. Those facts must come from registered public sources or approved catalog documents and carry capture dates and provenance.
